## Section 2: Payment Deadlines

Tuition fees for the odd semester must be paid by 10th August 2026 and for the even semester by 10th January 2027. A late fee of Rs. 500 per week applies after the deadline. Payments can be made online via the student portal.

## Section 5: Scholarships

The college offers merit-based scholarships, state government scholarships (for SC/ST/OBC/EWS categories), and a need-based fee waiver. Applications open on the National/State Scholarship Portal after admission confirmation; the college scholarship cell assists with document verification.
