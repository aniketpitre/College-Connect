## Section 1: Theory Exam Timetable

The odd semester theory examinations are scheduled from 25th November to 12th December 2026. The detailed subject-wise timetable will be released on the college notice board and student portal by 1st November 2026.

## Section 4: Revaluation & Backlog

Students may apply for revaluation within 10 days of result declaration through the exam portal, with a fee of Rs. 300 per subject. Backlog exams for failed subjects are conducted along with the next regular semester exam.
