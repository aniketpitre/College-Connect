## Section 1: Key Dates

The last date to submit the admission application for the 2026-27 academic year is 15th July 2026. Merit lists will be published on the college website in three rounds.

## Section 3: Required Documents

For undergraduate admission, applicants must submit the following documents:

- 10th and 12th mark sheets
- Transfer certificate
- Migration certificate
- Category / caste certificate (if applicable)
- Aadhaar card
- Passport-size photographs
- Online application fee receipt
